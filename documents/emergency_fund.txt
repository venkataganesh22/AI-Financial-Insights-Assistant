Emergency Fund Guide for Salaried Professionals and Students in India

What is an Emergency Fund?
An emergency fund is a dedicated pool of cash or highly liquid assets reserved strictly for unexpected financial crises, such as sudden job loss, medical emergencies, major appliance failure, or urgent home repairs.

How Much Should You Save?
- Rule of Thumb: 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (rent, groceries, utility bills, loan EMIs, basic insurance).
- Example: If your essential monthly living costs are ₹25,000, your target emergency fund should be between ₹75,000 and ₹1,500,000.
- For freelancers or individuals with volatile income, maintaining 6 to 12 months of expenses is recommended.

Where to Keep Your Emergency Fund?
1. High-Yield Savings Account: Instant liquidity via UPI and ATM access for instant emergencies.
2. Liquid Mutual Funds or Instant-Redemption Debt Funds: Higher returns than savings accounts with T+1 day withdrawal capability.
3. Short-Term Fixed Deposits (FD) with premature withdrawal option: Safe, predictable interest rates without long lock-ins.

What NOT to do with an Emergency Fund:
- Do NOT invest emergency funds in equity, stocks, or volatile mutual funds.
- Do NOT use emergency funds for festive shopping, gadget upgrades, or vacation travel.
- Keep the funds separate from your regular daily spending account to avoid accidental spending via UPI.
