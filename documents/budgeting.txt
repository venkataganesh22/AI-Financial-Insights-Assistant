Budgeting Guidelines and Spending Strategies

The 50/30/20 Rule:
A popular and simple rule for personal budgeting:
- 50% Needs: Essential expenses required for basic living, including rent, groceries (DMart, BigBasket), electricity, phone bills, insurance, and medical expenses.
- 30% Wants: Discretionary expenses that enhance lifestyle, including dining out (Swiggy, Zomato), streaming subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify), shopping (Amazon, Flipkart), movies, and vacation trips.
- 20% Savings & Investments: Debt repayment, building an emergency fund, and regular monthly SIPs into index funds or mutual funds.

Tracking Expenses:
- Categorize every transaction into Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Rent, Entertainment, Healthcare, etc.
- Monitor impulse buying on quick-commerce apps (Zepto, Blinkit) and food delivery apps (Swiggy, Zomato).
- Set monthly budget limits per category and review progress weekly.

Managing Overspending:
- Overspending occurs when discretionary expenses exceed target thresholds or when total spending leaves insufficient savings (<15-20% of net income).
- Automate savings by scheduling SIP transfers on the 1st or 5th of every month immediately after receiving salary.
- Maintain a 24-hour waiting rule before making non-essential purchases above ₹2,000.
